About Quick Fill (QF)

        
      

      

        
Quick Fill is an intelligent identity document parsing and KYC auto-fill application. It processes Aadhaar and PAN cards 100% locally on your machine with zero cloud dependence.

        
        

          

            Engine: Windows Media Native OCR + OpenCV Enhancement
          

          

            Supported Documents: Aadhaar Card (Front & Back), PAN Card
          

          

            Smart Merging: Combines data across multiple card scans without overwriting
          

          

            Privacy: Fully offline, zero data transmitted over external networks
